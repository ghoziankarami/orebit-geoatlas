## Perintah Utama

- **Cek status & gelombang WP:**
  - `python3 ops/scripts/agents/wp.py check`
  - `python3 ops/scripts/agents/wp.py status`
  - `python3 ops/scripts/agents/wp.py wave`
  - `python3 ops/scripts/agents/wp.py brief WP-01 --role executor`
  - `python3 ops/scripts/agents/wp.py workflow WP-01 WP-02 WP-03 WP-04 --exec-model flash --review-model pro`
- **Gerbang CI Lokal & Pengujian:**
  - `bash ops/scripts/ci/local-ci.sh --fast` (validasi cepat guard + referensi + unit test + build app)
  - `bash ops/scripts/ci/local-ci.sh --full --write-receipt` (sertifikasi lengkap termasuk Playwright desktop/mobile EN/ID + receipt exact-SHA)
- **Pipeline Data & Aplikasi:**
  - `make validate` · `make index` · `make fetch-esdm REGION=<wilayah>` · `make inventory REGION=<wilayah>` · `make tiles REGION=<wilayah>` · `make tiles-all` · `make dev` · `make app`

## Ringkasan Keputusan Arsitektur

- **Stack:** Vite + TypeScript + MapLibre GL JS + PMTiles (`app/`), Python/GeoPandas + tippecanoe (`pipeline/`). Tanpa backend server.
- **8 Wilayah Kepulauan Nasional:** `sumatra`, `babel`, `jawa`, `balinusra`, `kalimantan`, `sulawesi`, `maluku`, `papua` + bundel gabungan nasional (`all` / `indonesia`).
- **Harmonisasi & Kepercayaan Data:** `reference/sources.csv` → `reference/crosswalk.csv` → `reference/units.csv` (dengan umur Ma dari `ics_intervals.csv`, kelas litologi dari `lithology.csv`, dan kolom `confidence`: `curated` | `auto` | `flagged`) → `app/public/data/indonesia-units-index.json`.
- **Lisensi:** Kode MIT; data geologi ESDM/PSG mengikuti Lisensi Terbuka PSG (wajib atribusi, dilarang dijual).
- **Prompt Eksekusi Satu Kali:** Lihat [docs/handover/PROMPT-close-all-tasks.md](docs/handover/PROMPT-close-all-tasks.md).
